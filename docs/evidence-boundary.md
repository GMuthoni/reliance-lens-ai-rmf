# Evidence Boundary

This repository intentionally separates observed facts from analytical inference and testable hypotheses.

## Documented within this repository

- the defined employment-screening analytical fixture;
- the stated system purpose;
- the listed safeguards;
- the dependency relationships proposed for analysis;
- the disruption scenario;
- the candidate test protocol; and
- the candidate measurement constructs.

## Analytical inference

The following are analytical conclusions from the worked scenario, not observations of a real deployment:

- several safeguards may share material dependencies;
- the ranking system may acquire practical authority while remaining formally advisory;
- a shared dependency may create common-mode degradation across controls.

## Hypotheses

The following require empirical testing:

- whether operational independence can be measured reliably;
- whether different assessors produce sufficiently consistent assessments;
- whether candidate measures discriminate between system designs;
- whether dependency-aware testing reveals material information not captured by existing methods; and
- whether the results change risk-management or deployment decisions.

## Non-claims

This repository does not establish that Reliance Lens:

- is validated;
- improves outcomes;
- is superior to NIST AI RMF practices;
- is a NIST-approved framework;
- is suitable for certification; or
- generalizes across sectors without further study.
