## GOVERN

Reliance Lens supports governance by making responsibility for operational dependencies explicit.

Questions include:

- Who owns recruiter capacity?
- Who can suspend ranking?
- Who decides that review is no longer meaningful?
- Who owns candidate correction?
- Who records a loss of oversight?
- Who authorizes recovery?

## MAP

Reliance Lens expands system mapping beyond the model to include:

- the AI system;
- the affected workflow;
- human roles;
- safeguards;
- dependencies;
- fallback mechanisms;
- intervention authority;
- recovery paths;
- affected people; and
- channels for correction or appeal.

## MEASURE

Reliance Lens turns human-oversight claims into measurable or testable questions.

Examples:

- How much review capacity is required?
- What is the maximum tolerable queue delay?
- What threshold causes ranking to pause?
- Can reviewers change or reject model outputs?
- How long does correction take during degraded operation?
- Is fallback independent of the failed dependency?

## MANAGE

Reliance Lens supports management decisions by identifying which dependency should be strengthened first.

Potential interventions include:

- capacity thresholds;
- automatic ranking restrictions;
- independent review personnel;
- separate correction channels;
- alternate records;
- emergency decision authority;
- manual shutdown procedures;
- event logging; and
- recovery rehearsals.

## Distinctive contribution

The contribution is not another list of governance controls.

It is a way to examine whether controls remain available as a system under stress.
